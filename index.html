01 / UNDERSTANDING MENTAL HEALTH

What does mental health mean?

Mental health is about our emotional, psychological and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, relate to others and handle everyday challenges.

♡

Emotional well-being

Recognising feelings, expressing them in healthy ways and learning how to cope when life gets difficult.

♧

Relationships

Feeling connected, respected and able to build supportive relationships with friends, family and others.

☼

Everyday coping

Managing ordinary pressures, adapting to change and knowing when it is time to ask someone for help.

2 in 5

02 / THE PROBLEM

When stigma makes
silence feel safer

Stigma happens when people are judged, labelled or treated unfairly because of mental health challenges. In a school setting, it can make a student afraid to speak honestly or seek help.

  • ✓

    Name-calling and teasing: classmates may use hurtful labels instead of showing understanding.

  • ✓

    Fear of being judged: a student may worry that friends or teachers will think they are weak.

  • ✓

    Myths and misinformation: mental health challenges may be misunderstood or explained unfairly.

“A student who is struggling does not need a label. They need to be heard, respected and supported.”

04 / PRACTICAL SUPPORT

How can we make school a safer place?

Listening without judgement and helping a friend reach a trusted adult makes a real difference.

STEP 01

Listen carefully

Give the person time to speak. Do not interrupt, mock or rush to judge.

STEP 02

Take them seriously

Avoid saying “just get over it.” Their feelings deserve respect.

STEP 03

Encourage support

Suggest speaking with a trusted parent, teacher, or counselor.

STEP 04

Keep checking in

A simple “How are you doing today?” reminds someone they are cared for.
