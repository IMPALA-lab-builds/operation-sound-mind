## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted specifically for Kenyan secondary school students and young adults (ages 14–20) who are navigating academic stress like KCSE preparation, social expectations, peer relationships, and internal emotional conflicts. 

The personality is that of an empathetic older sibling or trusted companion: warm, completely stigma-free, non-clinical, grounded, and quietly hopeful. The interface eschews cold hospital aesthetics and stark clinical terminology in favor of conversational, human, and culturally resonant spaces.

The visual direction combines **Contemporary Tactile Warmth** and **Modern Soft Minimalism**:
- **Tactile Comfort:** Friendly, soft, organic card structures that feel safe and physically holding rather than detached or institutional.
- **De-escalating Whitespace:** Generous breathing room across layouts to consciously reduce cognitive overload, stress, and anxiety.
- **Approachable Modernity:** Clean mobile-first UX with familiar micro-interactions, pill-shaped tags, and comforting conversational prompts.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the natural Kenyan landscape—rich sun-baked soil, calm highland foliage, and sunlit savannah clay—engineered to reduce cortisol spikes and feel deeply grounding.

- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Warm Terracotta):** Represents vitality, heart, warmth, and safe guidance. Used for primary actions, supportive active highlights, and primary conversational focus points.
- **Secondary (`#4D7858` - Soft Forest Sage):** Evokes healing, steady breathing, mental clarity, and renewal. Deployed for grounding tools, coping exercise interfaces, and progress markers.
- **Tertiary (`#E0A96D` - Golden Ochre / Warm Amber):** Conveys quiet optimism, encouragement, and celebratory milestones without aggressive contrast.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF7F0` - Warm Sand Cream):** Replaces harsh stark whites with a comforting, warm, eye-friendly surface canvas that significantly reduces eye strain and reading fatigue.
- **Text & Structure (`#2A2421` - Deep Earth Cocoa):** Ensures AAA-compliant high readability while replacing clinical cold blacks with a softer, warm, organic darkness.

## Typography

The type system blends the warm, rounded geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and interactive labels with the inviting, legible clarity of **Be Vietnam Pro** for long-form reading, journal prompts, and reflection pieces.

- **Tone and Temperament:** Friendly, approachable, human, and clear. Avoid overly condensed or severe typefaces.
- **Hierarchy and Flow:** Use soft weight scaling (`600` and `700`) rather than aggressive black weights to prevent visual intimidation. 
- **Readability Rules:** Body text must strictly use generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless scanning for students reading through heightened anxiety or fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid structure is utilized to reflect the reality that the majority of Kenyan youth engage via handheld smartphones.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (up to 599px): 4-column fluid grid, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins.
  - Tablet (600px - 1023px): 8-column fluid grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) side margins.
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12-column constrained grid (maximum content container of 1120px) with 32px (`2rem`) gutters to maintain cozy, focused reading lines.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px/8px base scale. Layouts emphasize open vertical space (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between conversational modules to prevent claustrophobia, allowing youth to digest complex emotions one thought at a time.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** and **tonal layering**, completely avoiding cold, sharp grey drop shadows.

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Shadows are tinted with deep cocoa (`#2A2421`) at ultra-low opacity (4%–8%) with high diffusion blurs (16px–24px) and subtle vertical displacement. This makes cards appear to rest gently on the warm sand background like soft felt pads.
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Canvas: `#FBF7F0` (Warm Sand).
  - Resting Card Surface: Pure warm white (`#FFFFFF`) with a faint 1px outline tinted in primary earth tone at 8% opacity (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`).
  - Raised Interactive Surface: White with warm ambient elevation (`0 8px 24px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.06)`).
- **No Stark Borders:** Harsh 100% black boundaries or clinical dividers are strictly prohibited; dividers must always use a soft sand-cocoa wash (`rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is organic, soft, and protective, engineered to feel comfortable in the hand:
- Standard containers and modular content cards use smooth, rounded corners (`0.5rem` / `8px` or `1rem` / `16px`).
- Deep emotional interaction touchpoints, interactive tags, and conversation pill chips utilize full pill radius (`9999px`) to eliminate any harsh edge perception.
- Large floating containers and bottom modal sheets for crisis support or peer chats use generous curvature (`1.5rem` / `24px` on top corners) to deliver an embracing, non-threatening presentation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta background (`#C85A32`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), rounded-lg (12px) or full pill, subtle warm glow shadow on hover/tap. Never aggressive red.
- **Secondary (Calming):** Forest Sage background (`#4D7858`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), used for grounding sessions and mindful breathing routines.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with warm sand-cocoa outline (`1.5px solid rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.15)`) and cocoa text (`#2A2421`).

### Chips & Conversation Starters (Pill Tags)
- Full pill border radius (`9999px`).
- Default: Soft warm sand fill (`rgba(224, 169, 109, 0.15)`), cocoa text (`#2A2421`), and no border.
- Selected: Deep forest sage or terracotta fill with crisp, high-contrast white text.
- Interaction: Gentle spring scale animation on press to reinforce engagement.

### Cards
- Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`) over the warm sand canvas.
- Rounded corners (`16px`).
- Subtle ambient cocoa shadow (`0 4px 18px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.05)`).
- Generous internal padding (`20px` to `24px`) to frame stories, peer questions, and mood tracking.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface: Cream tint (`#F5EFE6`) or white.
- Border: `1.5px solid rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.12)`, transitioning to terracotta (`#C85A32`) upon active focus with a subtle 3px terracotta halo at 15% opacity.
- Placeholder: Muted warm cocoa (`rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.45)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded-md for checkboxes (6px), circular for radio buttons.
- Checked state fills with secondary forest sage (`#4D7858`) with a clear white check icon or center dot.

### Specialized Components
- **Daily Mood Check-In Dial:** A gentle arc-based or pill-button mood selector displaying expressive, stigma-free descriptors ("Overwhelmed with KCSE", "Steady", "Need to Vent", "Feeling Hopeful").
- **Direct Hotline & Peer Support Drawer:** Accessible with one tap, styled as a calming, warm cocoa banner that opens a soft sheet with toll-free Kenyan youth lines (e.g., Childline Kenya 116, Befrienders Kenya) without dramatic alarm colors.
- **Breathing Guide Ripple:** A cyclical expanding concentric circle in sage green to guide pacing through high exam-anxiety moments.
